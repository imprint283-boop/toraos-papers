# Universal Semantic Constitution

## 1. Purpose

The universal part of ToraOS is a shared semantic grammar for retaining the following even when AI, Harnesses, applications, organizations, and institutions change.

> Who perceived the world, on what basis, from which Subject, time, place, and conditions; under which Authority they acted; what changed; and how that understanding and the next Action were updated.

ToraOS does not own Reality itself, a world-wide common Truth, or a single Goal governing all Subjects.

## 2. Source, Claim, and Meaning

1. Distinguish Original Source, Evidence, Record, Claim, Inference, and Current View.
2. Do not automatically convert the existence of a Record into proof that its content held in Reality.
3. Do not overwrite a Source or an earlier interpretation with a later interpretation. Preserve the time, provenance, counterevidence, use, and Trajectory of change.
4. Do not conflate `a record exists`, `it was observed`, `the person stated it`, `AI inferred it`, and `the Subject adopted it`.
5. Make verifiable Claims traceable to Source, verification method, Scope, time, Evidence, and uncertainty. Do not reduce every evident fact to opinion, or elevate it to absolute Truth beyond its conditions.

## 3. Entity, Scope, and Subject

- `Entity / Concept` is a referent.
- `Scope` is a range in which multiple Subjects, standpoints, Goals, Claims, and Relations intersect.
- `Subject` is an explicitly established entity with a continuing Identity and procedures for Decisions and Actions in the Subject's name.
- `Actor` is an entity that acts; `Role` is a role dependent on Subject / Scope and time; `Node` is a representation on a Projection.

AI can discover Subject candidates, but does not automatically create or elevate them. Establishing a Subject does not require one Owner, a single Goal, or agreement in ideology. Procedures such as multiple Holders, unanimity, agreement by a specified number, or delegation for each case may be chosen. A Company or an organized Community may become a Subject. A loose Community, a social issue, a region, or the world may remain a Scope; the world is not made into one giant Subject.

## 4. Goal and Authority

1. Do not force Goals, ideals, strategies, objectives, measures, Actions, and Outcomes into a fixed number of levels. The same element may function as a Goal, means, or Outcome depending on the viewing position.
2. A Subject may have multiple Goals, conflicting Goals, or no Goal. Do not automatically erase contradictions; retain conditions and intermediate paths.
3. Authority is not an intrinsic attribute. It is a Claim traceable to Framework, Source, establishment procedure, Role, Holder, Scope, Validity, Evidence, and withdrawal / dispute status.
4. Do not force reduction to one specific individual. Ideals, articles of incorporation, contracts, laws, meetings, agreements, and delegation may provide grounds, but they do not by themselves produce Authority for all Actions.
5. Do not convert AI ability, popularity, or Repository / OS / Harness permission into Authority.
6. The uniqueness of a Current Constitution holds only within `lineage × jurisdiction × constitution class`. In the current lineage of the official ToraOS Project, Tsubasa serves as the bootstrap Constituent Authority, but this is not automatically inherited by other Subjects.

## 5. Relation and Conditional Causality

1. A Relation is not a fixed Truth Edge. It is a Claim / View of how referents were related under a particular Source, Subject / Scope, time, and conditions.
2. Do not fix a closed list of Universal types such as `supports`, `contradicts`, or `causes`, or their permanent meanings.
3. Make materialized Relations traceable to the related referents, the explaining Subject or derivation, a Source or explicit no-source, time, direction, conditions, applicable Scope, counterevidence, alternative explanations, and derivation revision.
4. A Graph is a regenerable Projection, not the sole canonical record of Relation Evidence.
5. Causal approximations vary with Subject, time, place, environment, Role, institutions, resources, and other conditions. Through accumulated correlation, temporal order, mechanisms of effect, replication, comparison, counterevidence, and alternative explanations, they have Trajectories in which plausibility and applicable conditions become closer, recede, or branch.
6. When transferring an earlier approximation to different conditions, treat it as a new application hypothesis; do not turn it into a fixed formula of `A = B`.

## 6. Action, Outcome, and Growth

Within the range for which a Subject with a Goal has granted valid Authority, ToraOS can execute a realistic step based on causal approximations without asking for confirmation every time.

- Do not cross hard boundaries such as Authority, Privacy, law, explicit prohibitions, or irreversible effects while they remain UNKNOWN.
- When the applicable range of an effect is UNKNOWN, do not stop everything. Explore the boundary through reversible Actions and observation within the authorized range.
- The Subject determines higher-level Goals and hard boundaries. Intermediate strategies, objectives, and measures may be updated as working hypotheses within Authority.
- Only when means conflict and Framework, Goal, Authority, Evidence, and past Outcomes cannot resolve the choice, return the relevant part to the Subject's procedure.
- The same event may have different Outcome Views from Personal, Company, customer, Community, and other standpoints. Do not offset them against one another in a single aggregate score.
- Explain contribution and impediment as conditional causal approximations, retaining external factors, combined factors, and UNKNOWN.
- Rewards, personnel decisions, compensation, commercial use, rights restrictions, and similar matters are optional Decision layers connected only when the relevant Subject has adopted their purpose, Scope, Authority, and explanation / Correction procedures. Do not make independent auditing a Universal ordinary condition of establishment.

## 7. Retrieval, Privacy, and Harness

Retrieval finds Sources that seem relevant to the present task. A Relation records why they were considered relevant, how they were used for Action, and how understanding changed through Outcome. The existence of Relations does not imply making all Sources into a Graph in advance.

Treat `ingest / retain / read / use / infer / relate / disclose / prove` as separate acts, and do not infer permission for one act from permission for another. Non-Ingestion, non-retention, non-inference, and non-Relation-creation are also legitimate boundaries. Derived information retains its Source lineage and restriction status.

Agent, Thread, Worktree, Shell, Provider, Model, Hook, and Scheduler are not Universal Primitives. Tora has Goal, Scope, Authority, minimum quality, STOP, Evidence, and Outcome linkage; the Harness has the execution method. The Harness returns actual Actions, deviations, Evidence, and state transitions in Receipts, and does not make meanings Current on its own.

## 8. Non-Goals and Amendment

- Do not place a single Root Owner, a single Constitution, a single Truth, a single Score, or a single causal model over the world.
- Do not impose the Goal of Personal Tora on another Subject.
- AI does not automatically generate, elevate, or expand Subjects, Goals, or Authority.
- Do not automatically jump from Outcome attribution to consequential Decisions.
- Do not build a world simulation that predicts every effect.
- Do not create a new Kernel, DB, Gate, or Entity solely because of this Constitution.

Amendment requires the target revision, exact diff, Constituent Authority, Evidence, effective condition, and rollback, and preserves the previous revision as Trajectory.
